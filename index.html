Department of Dravyaguna
Aristaka
Botanical Name :

  • Sapindus trifoliatus L

    • Family Name :

  • Sapindaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Ritha

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Reetha

  • English – Soapberry

    • Synonyms :

  • Phenila

  • Phenaphala


    • Habitat :

  • Found throughout India, especially in the sub-Himalayan tract and South India, Grows in deciduous forests and is also cultivated


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Medium to large deciduous tree with grey-brown bark

  • Leaves: Alternate, paripinnate, smooth, with lanceolate leaflets

  • Fruit: Round, yellowish-brown, fleshy drupe that becomes hard and shiny on drying


    • Useful Parts :

  • Fruit pericarp


    • Rasadi Panchaka :

  • Rasa - Katu, Tikta, Kashaya

  • Guna - Laghu, Tikshna

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Ushna


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Antimicrobial

  • Insecticidal

  • Expectorant


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Kushta : Fruit decoction is used externally

  • Kesha Roga (Hair Disorders): Fruit pericarp is used as a natural shampoo


    • Dose

  • Churna: 3–6 g

  • Kwatha : 50–100 ml

    • Formulation:

  • Arishtaka Churna

  • Arishtaka Taila
